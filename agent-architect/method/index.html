SOURCES & METHOD

A starting design you can inspect.

This tool connects 125 AI use cases from Build With AWS to six editorial architecture families. It offers an eleven-question finder, a downloadable architecture brief, and three incident missions.

The original library

Every entry preserves its original ID and links to its source article. The original diagrams are historical references; each entry also has a suggested starting design and current implementation guidance.

  • Customer experience
  • Internal knowledge and productivity
  • Workflow automation
  • Data and analytics
  • Compliance, security and governance

How the recommendation works

The finder uses explicit rules. Your objective, workflow, evidence, authority, continuity, priorities, implementation preference, specialist needs, impact and data sensitivity determine a starting family and pilot guidance.

The six families are deterministic automation, a grounded knowledge assistant, a workflow with model steps, a bounded tool-using agent, a workflow with an agent inside, and a coordinator with specialists. They are editorial categories, not a certification or a probability of success.

Matching cases respect the selected objective, area, authority and family. Related examples are labeled separately. A recommendation can have no exact matching example. Each brief explains prerequisites and links to implementation documentation.

How the missions work

The invoice, refund and security missions use explicit rules with four committed decisions and sixteen fact combinations each. Time and reviewer capacity are fictional exercise constraints. The game makes no live AWS calls and provides no measured cost or performance claims.

Shared runs replay decisions under the current rules. They do not overwrite your own attempt or count another person’s decisions toward your progress. A game outcome describes that exercise, not the suitability of an entire production architecture.

Data and measurement

Your questionnaire choices and saved mission progress stay in this browser. Downloaded briefs are generated on your device. A shared replay link contains the fictional mission decisions needed to replay it.

This site uses Google Analytics through the shared Build With AWS property to measure visits, completed recommendations, mission starts and outcomes, downloads, copied replay links and clicks to the newsletter. Custom events do not send your questionnaire answers, search text or replay payload. A newsletter click records interest; it does not confirm a subscription.
